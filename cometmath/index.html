How it works

From counting it out to just knowing it

  1. Choose an operation and a level

    Addition, subtraction, multiplication or division. The child starts where it feels right and moves on at their own pace.

  2. The child answers calmly

    No time runs out, so there is time to think. For more of a challenge, turn on a time limit.

  3. The answers stick

    The problems get harder step by step. In the end, the child knows the answer right away instead of counting on their fingers.

What kids learn

Math facts that stick, leaving energy for harder math

CometMath is made for kids aged 6–10 who are moving from counting on their fingers to knowing math facts and times tables by heart. Expert is a challenge for 10-year-olds.

Count faster

Once 8 + 5 and 7 × 8 stick, the child no longer has to count step by step and can put that energy into harder math.

Just the right challenge

The problems get harder with every level, at the child's own pace.

Tricky problems come back

The same problems return until they stick – and that earns medals.

Support when math feels hard

Short, playful sessions with instant feedback and the right answer after a mistake.
