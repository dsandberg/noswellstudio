How it works

From sounding out to reading fluently

  1. The word shows for a moment

    There is no time to sound it out letter by letter, so the child practices recognizing the whole word at once.

  2. The child types the word

    That shows the word was really read – and spelling gets practiced along the way.

  3. Less time, step by step

    With every level, the word shows a little shorter. Reading gets faster and more confident, until it comes naturally.

What kids learn

From first words to school subjects

RocketWords is made for kids aged 6–10 who are moving from sounding out words to recognizing them whole. Expert is a challenge for those who already read fluently.

Read faster

Recognizing whole words without sounding them out is the foundation of fluent reading.

Recognize words

The same common words come back, so they stick as sight words.

Support for reading difficulties

Short, playful sessions with clear letters and instant feedback can be a good complement for kids who find reading hard.
