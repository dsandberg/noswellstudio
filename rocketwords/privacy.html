Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026

RocketWords does not collect any personal data. The app has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it sends nothing over the internet.

What is stored on the phone

So the game can remember where you are, the app stores a few things on the device: settings (such as language and sound), records, medals and how far you have come. This data never leaves the device and we cannot see it. It is removed when the app is deleted, and it can also be reset in the app's settings.

No permissions

The app does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or anything else on the device.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple or Google may send us anonymous reports when the app crashes. They contain technical details about the device and the error, but nothing about who you are. We only use them to fix bugs. You can turn this off in your device's settings.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no tracking, and it loads nothing from other services. The free version in the browser stores settings and records in your browser, the same way the app does on the phone.

Children

RocketWords is made for children. Since the app does not collect any personal data, it does not collect any data about children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. The app will never start collecting personal data without this policy being changed first.
