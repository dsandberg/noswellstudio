Get started

Join the beta in three steps

  1. Get TestFlight

    TestFlight is Apple's own app for testing apps before they're released. It's free on the App Store.

  2. Join the beta

    On your iPhone or iPad, tap Join the beta on this page, then follow the steps in TestFlight to install RocketWords. If you're on a computer, scan the QR code with the camera on your iPhone or iPad.

  3. Play

    It's the same app that will be on the App Store. Play whenever it works for you, as much or as little as you like. Even a short session helps. TestFlight lets you know when a new version is ready to install.

What to test

Just play and look out for these

Does your child get the game?

Can your child get started on their own, or do they need help? Is anything hard to understand?

Is it fun?

Does your child want to play again? Is there anything they find boring?

Are the words a good fit?

Are the words at the right level for your child's age? Are there any words your child doesn't understand?

Does everything work?

Does anything freeze, look odd, or go wrong?
