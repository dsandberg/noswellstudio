The apps

RocketWords

A word shows for just a moment. Can you read it? Practice reading whole words.

Reading fluently means knowing words at a glance instead of sounding out each one. RocketWords trains exactly that: a word flashes up for just a moment, and the child types what they read. On every level, the word is shown for a little less time. Four difficulties with ten levels each, from short everyday words for brand-new readers to long words from school subjects.

CometMath

Plus, minus, times and divide. At your own pace; quick answers score the most.

When number facts and times tables come automatically, children no longer have to count step by step and can put their energy into harder problems. CometMath trains exactly that, at the child's own pace: no clock is ticking, but faster answers earn more points. Four operations with four difficulties each, and picture math for the youngest.

Who makes the apps

No Swell Studio is Daniel Sandberg, a software developer who has worked mostly with frontend for many years. He made RocketWords and CometMath together with his two kids, aged 6 and 8, who have played the games, come up with new ideas and helped shape them.

He got tired of kids' apps where the game takes over from the learning, and of all the ads and tracking that pull children's attention away. In his apps the task is the game: read the word and type it, work out the answer and type it. There is as little as possible to distract, and you can sit down and practice for a while.

  • No account
